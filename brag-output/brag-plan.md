# SpeakWise launch video: plan

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 41 s (the user asked for a full product demo of 30 to 60 s, for X and LinkedIn).
**Tone:** polished app-store walkthrough with one sharp hook. Confident, calm, specific. No jokes needed: the hook is the joke everyone recognises.

## Answers
- **What is it?** A free speaking coach that listens to your placement-interview answer and shows exactly what to fix.
- **Who for?** Indian college students facing campus placements (HR rounds, GDs, technical rounds), plus their placement cells.
- **What it does for them:** turns "I know the answer but it came out wrong" into specific, scored habits they can fix and re-test.
- **What sets it apart:** every score has a reason; STAR check; retry and compare; mock interview and GD modes; company packs; 7 languages; analysis on the device; free with no sign-up.
- **Most impressive claim:** "Every score tells you why", shown with real report UI.
- **Visual hook:** the dreaded question "Tell me about yourself." and an answer that starts "Um, so basically…", with the fillers lighting up in the app's own amber highlight.
- **Real UI shown:** practice setup (modes, chips, packs), deep-teal speaking stage with the live waveform and transcript, feedback report (ring + metric rows), STAR check and the before/now comparison, interview summary, GD counter-argument, placement-cell dashboard.
- **Share caption:** "Placement prep is mostly reading. Interviews are speaking. SpeakWise listens to your answer and tells you exactly what to fix."

## Angle
Start with the moment every student dreads, then show the product fixing it in one continuous flow: pick → speak → see why → retry → go further.

## Identity
The app's own tokens: deep teal `#042F2E`/`#063B39` background, teal `#2DD4BF`, amber `#FFB547`, light surfaces `#FFFFFF`/`#F2F5F4`, Nunito 800/900 display, system UI body. The app's own CSS (`css/app.css`) and component markup are used for every UI panel.
