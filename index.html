How scoring works

Measured numbers (pace, fillers, pauses, time, voice, eye contact) are counted directly and are reproducible. Estimated numbers (relevance, structure, vocabulary) are heuristics; treat them as guidance.

Structure checks where things happen, not just whether a phrase appears: the opening must come early and the conclusion at the end. Story questions (“Tell me about a time…”) are checked for STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Relevance counts the topic’s key ideas, and is capped if you only repeat the keywords. If you use the AI coach, its relevance verdict replaces the keyword estimate.

Benchmarks: 120 to 160 words a minute is a comfortable speaking pace; pauses over 2 s are treated as hesitation; filler targets follow common public-speaking rubrics. Speech recognition can mishear accents, so you can always correct the transcript and re-analyse.

Privacy & data

  • Recordings and camera frames are analysed on your device and never uploaded. A recording exists only until you close the page.
  • The face model file is downloaded once from Google and jsDelivr.
  • Speech is transcribed by your browser’s speech service (Chrome and Edge send audio to their provider for recognition).
  • Sessions, saved topics, resume questions and your placement-cell details are stored only in this browser’s localStorage. Your resume text is never stored.
  • If you press Rephrase my answer, or turn on AI follow-ups, your transcript text (never audio or video) is sent through this site’s server to Google’s Gemini API; the site does not store it. On Google’s free tier, Google may use submitted text to improve its products, so do not include private information.
  • The progress file for your placement cell contains scores, dates and categories only, and is shared only if you send it.
